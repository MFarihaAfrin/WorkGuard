Frequently Asked Questions

How many days of leave can I request?

Up to 3 days can be requested through the portal. For longer leave, please contact HR directly.

Do I need a document for Sick Leave?

Yes, a medical certificate is required when applying for Sick Leave.

How are expense claims approved?

Your Manager reviews and approves first, followed by a final approval from HR/Finance before payment is issued.

What receipt formats are accepted?

JPG and PNG images only, under 5MB.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WorkGuard?

WorkGuard is a web-based workplace management systemfor managing employee attendance, leave, expenses, approvals and reimbursements.

Who can use WorkGuard?

WorkGuard is designed for authorized Employees, Managers and HR/Finance users within the organization.

How do I register?

Employees must use the employee ID, company email and temporary password provided by the organization to complete registration.

I cannot log in. What should I do?

Please contact your organization's HR department for account or registration-related problems.

What features does WorkGuard provide?

WorkGuard provides attendance tracking, leave management, expense claims, receipt OCR, AI assisted risk analysis, approvals, notifications and reporting.

Where can I get help?

For account or workplace-related issues, contact your organization's HR department. For WorkGuard technical support, contact the WorkGuard support team.
